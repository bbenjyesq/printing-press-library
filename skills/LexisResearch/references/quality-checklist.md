# Quality Checklist (Phase 5)

Read the finished memo cold and run this list. Fix everything that fails before delivery.

## Citations
- [ ] Every authority named in the memo was retrieved from Lexis+ (`cite check` recognized it,
      under the name the memo uses) and carries a **VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED** tag. Nothing ships as
      authority while UNVERIFIED.
- [ ] Every load-bearing case has a **pin cite** read from the Lexis+ `[*page]` markers.
- [ ] Each case actually **holds** what the memo cites it for, in the **majority opinion** — not a
      dissent, concurrence, or Lexis headnote / case summary.
- [ ] One lead cite per proposition; no string-citing for volume.
- [ ] No authority appears that came only from CourtListener or web search.

## Quotations & statutes
- [ ] Every quotation passed `citecheck quote` with its pin; any change is signaled with brackets
      or ellipses.
- [ ] Every statute is confirmed to the subdivision against the Deering's copy, with the effective
      date / version noted.

## Shepard's
- [ ] Every cited case has a dated Shepard's signal in the Shepard's Status table.
- [ ] Every red-flagged case has a bottom line and a reason per flag, and is either dropped from
      load-bearing use or has its limits stated in the memo.
- [ ] No case is described as "good law"; the memo says "no Warning-level treatment" instead.

## Authority quality
- [ ] Binding / persuasive / distinguishable labeled honestly.
- [ ] No reliance on an uncitable unpublished California opinion (CRC 8.1115).
- [ ] Contrary and cautionary authority is surfaced, not dropped.

## Answer integrity
- [ ] The Short Answer matches what the verified authority supports. If a proposition has no
      clean support, the memo says so.
- [ ] The banner tallies match the memo.
- [ ] California law governs; for a federal forum, the federal analogs and choice of law appear.
- [ ] Attorney-work-product header present; the memo does not read as filed or final.
